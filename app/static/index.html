    How to read a result

    Probability is the ensemble's estimate that the patient has heart disease. The prediction applies the decision threshold, which was chosen on training data and favours catching disease (high sensitivity).

    Uncertainty comes from conformal prediction. Inside the amber referral band the model's 90% prediction set contains both outcomes, so the case is marked for clinician review instead of a single label.

    Explanations are SHAP values: each input's contribution, in percentage points, that moves the probability from the average training patient to this patient. They describe the model's reasoning, not medical causation.

    Research prototype for academic use. Not a medical device and not validated for diagnosis. Before use at a new hospital, recalibrate to that hospital's disease rate.

    SmartHeart is a research prototype. Predictions support, and do not replace, clinical judgement.
